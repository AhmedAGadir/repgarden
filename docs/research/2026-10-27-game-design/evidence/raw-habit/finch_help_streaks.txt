Streaks are a fun way to celebrate consistency and keep your self-care momentum going!

## 🔥 How Streaks Work:

*   Your streak is counted **each day you open the Finch app -** just checking in is enough!
*   The streak grows as long as you return to the app each day.

## 📅 What if I Miss a Day?

*   If you accidentally skip a day, don’t worry!
*   You can use **Rainbow Stones** to repair your streak.
*   You’ll also earn a **free Streak Repair Saver** every **3 adventures**, as long as you haven’t already reached the **maximum of 2** saved repairs!

## ⏸️ Need a Break? Try Pause Mode:

*   Planning to take a few days off - Use **Pause Mode** from the Settings page.
    *   While paused, your streak will be **safely frozen** and won’t be affected by your time away.

**Tip**: _Even a quick check-in helps keep your streak alive. Build your streak at your own pace and celebrate your progress!_
