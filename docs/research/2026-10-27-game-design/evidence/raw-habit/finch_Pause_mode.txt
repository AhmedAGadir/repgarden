Pause mode is a setting that allows you to:
* Preserve your [[Streaks]].
* Hide the [[widgets]] and stop notifications.
* Not allow people to give you [[Good Vibes]] or make you a Goal Buddy.

Go to the top left icon and scroll down to Account to find Pause mode. You can choose to be back in any number between 1 to 7 days (default is 3 days.) You can turn on a Reminder to check in.
