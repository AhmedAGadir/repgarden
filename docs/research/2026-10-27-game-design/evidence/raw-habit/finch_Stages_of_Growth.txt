Your [[Birb]] will go through 5 stages of life and grow up as you achieve full [[Energy]] for a day and go on an [[Adventuring|Adventure]]. As it reaches each stage it will increase in size, and different features will become available for color customization. The [[Energy|energy]] required to start an Adventure will be slightly higher, but the time length of an Adventure will decrease 

Start accumulating [[Energy]] by completing [[Goals]] and sending [[Good Vibes]]. Nearly everything on the [[Activities]] lists will earn some amount of energy; you can add them to your Goals or do them right away. Categories include writing [[Reflections]], doing guided [[Breathe|Breathing]] exercises, listening to [[Soundscapes]], as well as [[Movements]], [[Timers]], [[Act of Kindness]], and using [[First Aid]]. 

After you achieve full energy and the Adventure begins, any additional energy from goals, good vibes and activities will decrease the duration of your journey. (A typical 5 energy goal reduces the time by 10 minutes: 2 minutes per energy.) It will also earn [[Rainbow Stones]]!
==Stages==
:''See also: [[Colors|Parts of the Body]]''
===Egg===
When a user starts using Finch, they pick an egg that will hatch into a birb. Users can choose between a Blue, Orange, Pink, Green, Purple, or Gray egg. This color will also be the color of the baby birb once it hatches. The users can then hatch the egg. This happens when you first get finch

=== Baby ===
The egg hatches into a baby birb. The user can choose the birb's pronouns, name, and trait. 

A baby birb is one color with a yellow beak and gray feet. A baby birb can only travel in [[Finchie Forest]] and cannot change its appearance.

However, the user can change a baby birb's [[clothing]] or the birbhouse [[furniture]] with items collected from [[Seasonal Events]] or items purchased from [[Mr. Prickles' Shop]] and [[Finkea Furnishings]]. 

While the birb is a baby, it takes 15 energy is go on an Adventure. An Adventure lasts around 8 hours. The baby stage lasts 7 adventure days. 

===Toddler===
After 7 adventures, the birb grows into a toddler. At this point, the birb grows a little larger, its headpatch and wings turn gray, and its beak and body can be customized. When the birb becomes a toddler, the user is prompted to choose a random color for the beak. [[The Color Studio]] also unlocks, allowing the user to purchase beak color [[Colors|dyes]] and body color dyes.

While the birb is a toddler, its takes 20 energy to go on an Adventure. An Adventures lasts around 7 hours. The toddler stage lasts 15 adventure days. 

===Child===
After 22 adventures, the birb grows into a child. At this point, the birb grows a little larger, and its headpatch and wings can be customized. When the birb becomes a child, the user is prompted to choose a random color for the headpatch and wings. The Color Studio also further unlocks, allowing the user to purchase headpatch color dyes and wings color dyes.

In addition, [[Travel with Sass]] unlocks, allowing the birb to travel to different [[locations]].

While the birb is a child, its takes 25 energy to go on an Adventure. An Adventure lasts around 6 hours. The child stage lasts 20 adventure days.

===Teen===
After 42 adventures, the birb grows into a teen. At this point, the birb grows a little larger, and its cheeks and feet can be customized. When the birb becomes a teen, the user is prompted to choose a random color for the cheeks and feet. The Color Studio also further unlocks, allowing the user to purchase cheeks color dyes and feet color dyes. 

While the birb is a teen, it takes 30 energy to go on an Adventure. An Adventure lasts around 6 hours. The teen stage lasts 25 adventure days.

=== Adult===
After 67 adventures, the birb grows into an adult. At this point, the birb grows a little larger, and its tummy can be customized. When the birb becomes an adult, the user is prompted to choose a random color for the tummy. The Color Studio also finishes unlocking, allowing the user to purchase tummy color dyes.

When the birb is an adult, its takes 35 energy to go on an Adventure. An Adventure lasts around 6 hours. The adult stage is the final stage of growth.
