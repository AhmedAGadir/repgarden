'''Micropets''' are companions your Finch can own to accompany them on their adventures. Each micropet has interesting walking and standing movements, as well as a sleep pose. And each has their own unique "voice!" 

As of October 2026, there are 66 micropets available.

==Obtaining Micropets==
There are five ways to get new micropets; the main one is through [[Micropets#Hatching Micropets from Eggs|hatching]] random eggs from [[Professor Oat's Lab]]. Each [[Seasonal Events|seasonal event]] also gives a theme-specific pet. If you [[Invite|invite]] three friends into Finch, you will receive a micropet named [[Cookie the Cow]] (but you may still receive Cookie as a random egg.) If you initially join Finch through an invite from a friend, a selected micropet egg will be included, but that can only be done once. A another way is through gifting. 

=== Seasonal Events===
Users can collect a themed micropet on Day 25 of a seasonal event, or alternatively Day 20 for [[Finch Plus]] Members. After the event, the pet may be received in a random egg from Professor Oat, in any of its three color variants. Some pets like [[Raven the Cat]], [[Cheshire Cat]] and [[Toto the Dog]] are only available in one default color as they are characters. [[Krypto (micropet)]] is the first micropet to be limited and is not be available to hatch in eggs as the [[Supergirl]] event ended. However he is still obtainable through gifting. Similarly [[King Truffle Wuffle the Duwende]] wont be available to hatch from eggs and was only obtainable during the [[Forgotten Island]] event. Afterwards, you can still receive them through gifting.

===Hatching Micropets from Eggs===
:''Main article: [[Professor Oat's Lab]]'' 
A micropet egg must be be linked to a [[Goals|goal]] through [[Professor Oat]] in order to grow enough to hatch into a new micropet. After the goal is completed 7 times, a random micropet will hatch from the egg. Only one goal can be linked at a time. If you decide to change the linked goal, the growth will be reset to zero. After the egg is hatched, you can [[Professor Oat's Lab|receive a new random egg]], and choose a goal to link to it. Duplicate eggs may later be received. There is no limit to duplicates, but the developers are looking into changing the duplicate algorithm.

==Settings== 
Each individual micropet has its own settings. Go to the "Micropets" interface in your bag, and click on the pet. The gear symbol in the top right opens the settings. The default name and pronouns can be edited here, and if it can grow in age. While the micropet is still a baby, there is a toggle for "growable to adult." There is also an option to release the micropet. However even if you release the micropet, you will be able to hatch it again in the future.

==Stages of Growth==
Micropets hatch from an egg as a baby. After the micropet has gone on 7 adventures, it will grow into an adult. Micropets can remain "forever babies" by clicking the micropet, opening its settings, and toggling off the "growable to adult" option. This option goes away when the micropet is an adult.

==Appearance==
Micropets usually come in three color variations: their original color, plus two extra colors. A micropet egg earned from a seasonal event will be the "original" color. When eggs are obtained through Professor Oat, they will hatch a random micropet in one of its three color variants. Adult micropets are larger in size and have some changes in appearance compared to their baby counterparts. Pictures of the adult and baby micropets, in their three variant colors, are included on each micropet page. (See links in collapsed tables on this page.)

==Nature==
Micropets may also have a Nature. Known Natures include but are not limited to: Cheerful, Gentle, Quiet, Loyal, Playful, and Brave. They do not have any other known individuality. A micropet's nature is random and is not related to its color. Natures make each micropet unique and special.
