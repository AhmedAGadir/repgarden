# RepGarden build outline

This is a provisional product outline, not a lesson sequence or permission to implement everything. Epics record actual work and evidence as we build.

1. Workspace and project context — docs/epics/2026-09-19-01-workspace.md
2. Repository history and maintenance — docs/epics/2026-09-19-02-git.md
3. Design workspace and prototypes — docs/epics/2026-09-19-03-figma.md
3a. Scope and research — docs/epics/2026-10-26-04-scope-and-research.md
4. Learn Benji's clients — ask Benji what he knows about them: who they are, what happened just before they started looking for a trainer (the trigger), the outcome they want, what worries them before they sign up and who else they considered. His answers feed the branding, the landing page, the copy and the app's onboarding, and later a short build-series article on triggers. We ask Benji, not his clients.
5. Branding — generate options and vegetable mascot styles, choose a direction and create a small brand pack.
6. User flows and wireframes — main trainer/client journeys and feedback.
7. Architecture and project scaffolding — the stack was chosen on 26 October 2026 (docs/mvp-scope.md); scaffold the monorepo and explain the tradeoffs.
8. Accounts, trainer/client isolation and invitations.
9. Exercise library — Benji's demonstration videos with fallback images. (The AI content pipeline moved to later.)
10. Workout assignment, logging and progress.
11. Bookings and session payments — moved out of the MVP on 26 October 2026; kept here as a later step.
12. Mascot progression and mobile reminders.
13. Verification, operational tools, web deployment and support/privacy/deletion pages.
14. iOS and Android release — approval and any review-driven fixes.

Branding and implementation belong in this repository as they are produced. Standalone guides may reuse demonstrated methods; there is no maintained learner starter or prescribed completion path.
